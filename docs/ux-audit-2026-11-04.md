## Decision rationale to retain

Local saving, explicit playback, distinct microphone states, and partial-upload recovery were strengths to preserve. The next investment was clearer completion and recovery, rather than copying cloud defaults or treating an editor as cosmetic polish.

The eleven proposals covered active-transfer navigation, early deletion feedback, actual scheduler observations, exact saved-take review, library crowding, input preflight, storage/sharing organization, Drive outcomes, audience status, shortcut discovery, and source-preserving editing. Their implementation and acceptance evidence belong to PR #29, rather than a duplicate checklist here.

Several distinctions motivated them:

- Cancellation already existed. The upload problem was finding its controls from the library.
- Saving an archive destination does not establish a running scheduler. An upload command succeeding does not establish verified audience permissions or completed processing.
- Library crowding was initially a source-based concern because older captures lacked the new Drive/row actions. It was not initially a reproduced clipping defect. Later [integration evidence](pr26-ui/README.md) recorded clipping; [follow-through captures](ux-follow-through-images/README.md) recorded the layout repair.
- A saved take should lead to that exact final named file and stay silent until Play. Automatic upload must retain its separate consent and recovery rules.
- Screen/camera composition and mixed audio are baked into existing recordings. Later reframing needs preserved independent tracks and a format decision; a label change cannot provide it. [Editing constraints](editing-projects.md) retain the current boundary.
